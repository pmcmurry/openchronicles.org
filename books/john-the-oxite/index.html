About this work

John the Oxite’s first discourse is a formal address of thanksgiving, frank reproof, and counsel: the patriarch thanks God for the emperor’s safe return, argues that reverse of fortune after the accession has a cause, catalogues fiscal and ecclesiastical pressures he links to divine wrath, and presses repentance, just rule, restitution, and care for the churches.

The English rests on Lavriotes’s 1900 print from the lost Great Lavra manuscript, collated with Gautier’s 1970 re-edition. It is not a stenograph of a 1091 performance. Speech 2 is not included.

Translated from the Greek with AI assistance; edited by Paul McMurry.
